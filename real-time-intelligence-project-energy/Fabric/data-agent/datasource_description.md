Real-time data on the German power grid (source: Energy-Charts.info / Fraunhofer ISE, prices: Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de), collected since October 2026 and kept permanently:
- public net power generation per energy source, load, residual load and renewable share (15 min)
- Energy-Charts traffic light (green/yellow/red/grid congestion) incl. forecast
- day-ahead electricity prices DE-LU (15 min), incl. tomorrow from about 13:00
- physical cross-border flows with the 11 neighbouring countries (15 min)
- grid frequency of the Continental European grid (per second and per minute)
Use it for questions about electricity generation, renewables, prices, imports/exports and grid frequency in Germany.
